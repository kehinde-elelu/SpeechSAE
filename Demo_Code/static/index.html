THE PROBLEM

A voice becomes
a list of numbers.

At every short time frame, HuBERT represents speech with 768 numbers. Those coordinates can mix information about pronunciation, voice, and context. Reading a single coordinate rarely gives us a simple explanation.

What is a hidden activation?

It is the value of one coordinate inside the model at one frame. It describes an internal representation, rather than the audio waveform.

THE LENS

More features.
Fewer active at once.

A sparse autoencoder represents those 768 coordinates using a dictionary of 6,144 possible features. Only a small fraction respond at each frame. We can inspect where those responses occur and listen for a shared pattern.

Sparse does not automatically mean interpretable. A feature needs evidence before it gets a name.

How to read these results

What are features?

Learned patterns in HuBERT’s internal representation. Most have no verified name.

Activation vs. firing frequency

Activation is response strength. Firing frequency is the fraction of frames with a nonzero response.

What does a peak mean?

The strongest response for one feature in this recording. An inactive feature has no peak time.

What does this explain?

HuBERT representations. This does not produce a transcript, a MOS score, or proof of a feature’s meaning.
